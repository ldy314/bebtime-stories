小种子芽芽和书签的故事（胎教期）

妈妈的花园里，一粒小种子芽芽裹着暖暖的土，耐心地等着自己发芽。它轻轻说：再等等，就快了。

啪嗒啪嗒，一只小蜗牛爬过书页，留下亮亮的水痕。芽芽从土里望出去，看见一张书签正躺在故事书里，静静看着一行行字。

书签悄悄告诉芽芽：我见过一个不会写字的男孩，他用画来讲故事；我也见过一个小朋友，把睡前故事的温度，一页页折成了小船。滴滴答答，是雨点在窗外给故事打拍子。

喵喵，隔壁的小猫凑过来看书，把毛茸茸的尾巴当书签夹了进去。芽芽忍不住笑了：原来编故事的快乐，连小猫也想分一口。

圆圆的月亮升起来，安安静静陪着这一页一页的世界。月光守候：它像一盏小夜灯，把影子照得软软的，好让每个故事都睡得安稳。

晚安，小宝宝，月亮陪着你。等你出来了，芽芽也发芽了，我们一起，把今天的故事，讲给你听。

✨ 故事小语
每一个故事都像一粒种子，被温柔地讲出来，就会在心里悄悄发芽。

Dewdrop Postman's Goodnight Letter (Prenatal)

On a petal at dawn lived a lively little dewdrop named Lulu, full of questions. Each morning she cried: a letter's here!

Tonight her letters were all about home. Flutter went the curtain as Daddy snored a low, rumbling tune — boom, boom, like a far-off train. Ding-dong, the dinner bell rang, and stories tumbled out between bites of warm rice.

Mama's catchphrase floated through the house like a hug: 'careful, sweet, I'm right here.' Lulu folded it into a tiny envelope and tucked it by the baby's ear.

She gathered them all into one warm embrace — a ring of light like open arms, holding the little one steady, as if in a soft nest. Little one, can you feel how many hands wrote this letter just for you?

Whisper, whisper, the house grew quiet. Lulu sealed the goodnight kiss with a drop of moonlight and slid it under the door of the tummy.

Good night, little one. The mail of love is delivered every day, and you are its favorite address.

✨ 故事小语
Love arrives in tiny daily messages — a kiss, a story, a snore — and you are already part of the family's warmest mail.
