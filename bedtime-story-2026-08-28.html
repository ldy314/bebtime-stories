云朵朵朵和心跳的小鼓（胎教期）

云端小精灵朵朵，最爱飘呀飘去照顾每一个人。今晚，她飘到妈妈肚子边，听见里面有一面小小的鼓。

呜呜，晚风轻轻吹过；滴答滴答，那是妈妈心跳的小鼓，一下一下，稳稳地敲着。朵朵把耳朵贴上去，觉得又暖又安心。

沙沙沙，窗外的树也睡着了，叶子互相蹭着，像在说晚安。朵朵把一捧云朵轻轻盖在妈妈肚子上，像盖一床软软的被子。

小宝宝，你听见那面小鼓了吗？咚、咚、咚，那是妈妈在替全世界跟你说：我在呢，别急。

朵朵拉着一缕月光，在妈妈肚子上织了个小摇篮。她想：等宝宝出来的那天，这面小鼓会一直响，一直陪着他。

晚安，小宝宝，月亮陪着你。你被好好地抱着，慢慢地、甜甜地，长大就好。

✨ 故事小语
你一直被温柔地抱着、听着爱的声音长大，什么都不用怕，慢慢来。

Brown Bear's Shared Lunch (Prenatal)

Little brown bear An-an lived in a cozy forest hollow, soft and slow and full of calm. One quiet evening he packed a tiny shared lunch.

He padded along, pat-pat, pat-pat, humming a purr of a tune. A grandmother squirrel offered him a seat; an old tortoise shared a crust of warm bread. Kindness, he learned, was just passing things along.

An-an thought of the baby in Mama's tummy and wanted to share something too. He gathered a cradle of stars — huff... lu... he blew them gently upward, where they held hands and rocked.

The stars sang a wordless song, dum-dum, dum-dum, like a lullaby without words. Little one, can you hear the gentle rocking? That is the whole forest saying you belong.

He left the shared lunch by the moonlit path: a crumb of bread, a whisper of fur, a handful of starlight. Someone tiny would find it soon.

Good night, little one. The world is kind, and you are already held in more love than you can yet know.

✨ 故事小语
Kindness is something we can share even in the smallest ways — and you are already so loved.
