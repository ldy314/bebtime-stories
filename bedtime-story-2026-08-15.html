小闹钟想睡懒觉（胎教期）

窗台上住着一只小闹钟，名字叫叮当。每天天一亮，它就叮铃铃、叮铃铃地响，把全家人都叫起来。它觉得自己可神气啦。

可是今天早晨，叮当忽然不想响了。它把铃声含在嘴里，咕噜咕噜的，就是不肯吐出来。它想：让我也赖一会儿床吧，就一小会儿。

妈妈走过来，没有生气，反而轻轻笑了。妈妈说：叮当，你累啦？那就歇一歇，妈妈先给你倒一小杯温水，润润嗓子。

叮当咕噜咕噜喝了几口，嗓子舒服多了。它听见爸爸在院子里沙沙沙地扫叶子，听见小鸟在窗外啾啾啾地唱歌，听见宝宝在妈妈肚子里扑通扑通地翻了个身。

小宝宝，你听见了吗？那是爸爸的笑声，从院子里传进来，像春天的雷一样，让人安心。原来睡醒以后的世界，这么热闹，又这么温柔。

叮当忽然觉得自己有劲儿了。它清清嗓子，叮铃铃、叮铃铃地响起来，比哪一天都响得开心。

宝宝呀，等你想睡懒觉的时候，也可以像叮当一样，先歇一歇，再开开心心地醒过来。早安，小闹钟。早安，小宝宝。

✨ 故事小语
想休息的时候可以休息一下，就像爸爸妈妈累了会歇一歇。可该醒的时候，还是要开开心心地醒过来。

The Little Alarm Clock Sleeps In (Prenatal)

On the windowsill lives a little alarm clock named Dingdong. Every morning, he rings ding-a-ling, ding-a-ling, and wakes the whole house. He feels very proud.

But this morning, Dingdong does not want to ring. He holds the bell in his mouth — gurgle, gurgle — and will not let it out. Just a little more sleep, he thinks, just one tiny bit.

Mama comes over, and instead of being cross, she smiles. She says: Dingdong, are you tired? Then rest a while. Let me pour you a little cup of warm water first.

Dingdong drinks — gurgle, gurgle — and his throat feels better. He hears papa sweeping leaves in the yard, swish-swish. He hears the birds chirping, tweet-tweet. He hears the baby turn over in mama's belly, thump-thump.

Little one, can you hear it? That is papa's laugh drifting in from the yard, low and warm like distant thunder. The waking world is so busy, and so gentle.

Suddenly Dingdong feels full of energy. He clears his throat and rings — ding-a-ling, ding-a-ling — happier than ever before.

Little one, when you want to sleep in, you may rest a little, then wake up happy again. Good morning, Dingdong. Good morning, little one.

✨ 故事小语
It is okay to rest when you are tired, just like grown-ups pause to catch their breath. And when it is time, you can wake up happy again.
