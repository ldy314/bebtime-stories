小闹钟叮当的一次早晨旅行（胎教期）

清晨，小闹钟叮当没有急着喊醒谁。它把响铃轻轻按住，悄悄从窗台上跳下来，跟着一缕阳光，出发了。小宝宝，天亮了，你也在妈妈肚子里醒来，伸个懒腰了吗？

阳光带它走过小区的石板路。叽叽喳喳，几只麻雀在花坛边开会，商量今天去哪棵树上唱歌。一只胖麻雀看见叮当，好奇地问：「小闹钟，你要去哪儿呀？」「我去看看早晨的世界，」叮当说，「我天天叫醒别人，还从没好好看过清晨呢。」

阳光又带它来到公园。小草上挂着亮晶晶的露珠，叮当轻轻一碰，露珠就骨碌碌地滚下去，像一颗小小的玻璃珠。「哇！」叮当晃呀晃，差点也跟着滚下去。「小心呀，」小草用叶子轻轻托住它，「露珠想玩滑滑梯呢。」

河边，一只白鹭正在散步，把长长的脖子一伸一伸的，像一个慢悠悠的老先生。「早安，小闹钟，」白鹭说，「你今天不用上班吗？」「我今天给自己放个假，」叮当说，「原来早晨这么好看呀。」白鹭点点头：「是呀，慢慢走，才能看见好看的。」

阳光越走越高，叮当开始想家了。它想窗台上的小窝，想妈妈每天早晨那声温柔的笑。「我该回去了，」叮当说，「不然大家该找不到时间啦。」阳光把它轻轻送回去，一路上，风唱着啦啦啦的歌，把叮当的铃铛吹得叮叮当当地响。

回到窗台的时候，家里已经有人醒了。妈妈正轻轻哼着歌，用手捂着肚子，对肚里的小宝宝说：「早上好呀，新的一天开始了。」叮当看见了，把自己的响铃收得好好的，只发出很小很小的一声「叮」，像在说：早安，小宝宝，今天也是亮晶晶的一天。

晚上，叮当躺进窗台上那床云朵一样软的小被子里，把自己裹成一个小小的、暖暖的茧。它想：原来清晨的世界这么好看，等小宝宝长大了，我要带它一起去看看。现在，先安心长大吧，小宝宝，清晨一直在等你。

✨ 故事小语
每个清晨都是一场小小的旅行，走一走，看一看，世界就装进了心里。

Sprout the Seed's Morning Journey (Prenatal)

Sprout the little seed lay in Mama's garden, warm and snug in its tiny bed of soil. "Just a little longer," it whispered, as it did every morning. But this morning, a warm breeze swept by and said, "Sprout, come and see the morning with me. It won't take long — you can come back to your bed."

So Sprout stretched up on tiptoe, and the breeze carried it over the garden. "Look," said the breeze, "there is the morning dew." Plip-plop went the dew on a leaf, and Sprout watched it roll, round and bright, like a tiny glass ball. "Oh," said Sprout, "it looks like a little mirror for the sun."

The breeze carried Sprout to the meadow, where a rabbit was hopping, soft and slow. "Good morning, little seed," said the rabbit. "Why are you not in your bed?" "I am having a morning adventure," said Sprout. "But I will go home before the sun gets too tall."

The breeze carried Sprout over the river, where the water went swish, swish, swish against the stones. "The river is singing," said Sprout. "Boom-boom," went the big stone in the middle, "I am the river's drum!" Sprout laughed a tiny seed laugh, light as a feather.

The sun climbed higher, and the breeze said, "It is time to go home." So the breeze carried Sprout back to Mama's garden and tucked it into its warm little bed. "Thank you," said Sprout. "Now I know what the morning looks like. Just a little longer, and one day I will reach up and see it every day."

That evening, from far away came a low, gentle laugh — Daddy's laugh, warm and reassuring like spring thunder. Sprout felt it in the soil and smiled. "Little one in Mommy's tummy," it whispered, "can you hear that laugh? That is love, the way it sounds."

Little one, while you grow safe and sound, you are like Sprout in its warm bed: waiting, but not empty. The morning, the dew, the rabbit, the singing river, and the warmest laughs of the people who love you — they are all waiting for you too. Just a little longer, and the world will open for you like a flower.

✨ 故事小语
Patiently waiting, like a seed in spring, is how the loveliest things unfold.
