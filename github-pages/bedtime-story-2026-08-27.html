小星星暖暖的星光摇篮（胎教期）

圆滚滚的小星星暖暖，最爱眨着眼睛在夜空里散步。今晚，它想给还没出生的小宝宝编一个摇篮。

叮咚叮咚，暖暖敲了敲云朵的门，请来一朵软软的云当摇篮底。滴滴答答，远处的小溪也送来一捧清凉的月光，铺在云上当作小被子。

一只小猫蹲在屋檐下，喵喵地仰头看，好像也想来坐坐。暖暖笑着邀它一起，星光就把它们轻轻圈成一个小小的圆。

星星们手拉手，围成一个温暖的摇篮，慢慢摇着、摇着，像在哼一首没有词的歌。小宝宝，你感觉到那轻轻的摇晃了吗？

暖暖凑近妈妈的肚子，小声说：我陪着你呀。不管黑夜多长，总有一颗小星星，专门为你亮着。

晚安，小宝宝，月亮陪着你。等你准备好了，这艘星光摇篮，会摇着你，去看世界上所有软软的梦。

✨ 故事小语
你从来都不是一个人，温柔的光和陪伴一直都在，安安心心地长大就好。

Conch Shell's Gentle Treasure (Prenatal)

In the deep-sea seagrass forest lived a little white conch named Swirl, quiet and full of curiosity. Tonight she set out to find a treasure.

She drifted along, sway-sway, sway-sway, held by the soft current like a baby in gentle arms. Ding-dong, a far bell-fish rang, and hush, the water hushed her name.

Swirl met a slow turtle, a napping cat-fish, and a lamb-soft cloud of bubbles. Each one gave her a tiny gift: a blue, a hum, a warm patch of light.

She gathered them into a blanket of color — the orange of dusk, the gold of wheat, the blue of the sea — and carried it all the way to Mama's tummy.

There she tucked it around the baby: the warmth of a blanket, a cloud-soft cocoon. Little one, can you feel how many friends helped make this for you?

Swirl curled at the edge of the wave and whispered: treasure found. Good night, little one, the sea is rocking you, safe and sound.

✨ 故事小语
The gentlest journeys lead to the warmest surprises — and you are loved on every quiet path.
