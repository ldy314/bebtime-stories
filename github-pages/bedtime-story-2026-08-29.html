小熊安安和会数数的向日葵（胎教期）

小熊安安慢吞吞地走出树洞，呼……噜……他要到暖洋洋的白天里去散散步。草叶上挂着露珠，簌簌地落下来。

扑通，一颗松果掉进小水坑，荡开一圈圈温柔的波纹。安安蹲下来看，觉得这水坑像一面小镜子，照出蓝蓝的天。

前面是一大片向日葵田。安安数啊数，发现每朵花里的籽都排成转圈圈的小路——一枚、两枚、三枚、五枚，越数越有意思。原来向日葵也会数数呀。

风轻轻吹，滴答滴答，是远处屋檐下妈妈在哼歌。那哼歌顺着阳光飘过来，软软地裹住安安的耳朵。小宝宝，你听见了吗？那是妈妈在替整个世界，给你唱早安。

安安把一片向日葵叶子顶在头上，慢吞吞地往回走。暖阳晒着背，他满足地舒了口气：今天的世界，亮晶晶的，真好。

等你准备好了，小宝宝，这些会数数的花、会唱歌的风，都会在门外，轻轻地等你。

✨ 故事小语
世界很大很奇妙，慢慢走、慢慢看，每一件小东西里都藏着让人惊喜的秩序与爱。

Mama Moon and the Spinning World (Prenatal)

Guardian Mama Moon floated in the night sky, gentle and full of wisdom. She leaned down to the round little tummy and said: sleep safe, Mama is here.

By daylight she took the baby on a quiet journey. Tick-tack went the little clock of the earth as it turned; tweet sang a small bird who had never flown, only practiced. Pat-pat went tiny feet exploring a sunlit path.

They visited the stone statues' dream on Easter Island, where old stone faces smiled in their sleep. They floated inside a dictionary, where every word was a small warm lamp lighting the way home.

And all along, beneath it all, came the drumbeat of a heartbeat — thump, thump, Mama's own. Little one, can you hear it? The whole spinning world keeps time with that same gentle drum.

They shared a peaceful lunch on a cloud: a crumb of light, a sip of blue. The baby, still unseen, grew calm and curious in the warm, turning day.

When the sun leaned low, Mama Moon tucked the light around the tummy and whispered: you are held by the whole round world. Sleep safe, little one, sleep safe.

✨ 故事小语
The world turns with a steady, loving rhythm — and that same rhythm beats inside you, from the very first day.
