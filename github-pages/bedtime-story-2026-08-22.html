小猪的泡泡浴（胎教期）

小猪哼哼最讨厌洗澡啦。一到洗澡时间，它就哼哼唧唧地往床底下钻，说：我不要洗澡，我不要洗澡！

可是这一天，妈妈在浴缸里放了好多好多泡泡。白白软软的泡泡堆得像小山一样高，还飘着香香的、甜甜的橘子味。

哼哼探出半个脑袋，眼睛一下子亮了。它咕噜咕噜地爬进浴缸，泡泡哗啦哗啦地涌上来，把它裹成了一个圆滚滚的泡泡猪。

它用鼻子拱起一团泡泡，噗——地吹了一口气，泡泡飞起来，在浴室里飘呀飘。妈妈在一旁看得咯咯直笑。

小宝宝，你听见了吗？那是爸爸在浴室门口笑呢，声音低低的、暖暖的，像春天的雷，让人听了心里踏踏实实。

洗完澡，哼哼被一条大毛巾裹得严严实实，香喷喷的，软乎乎的。它忽然觉得，洗澡也没那么讨厌嘛。

宝宝呀，等你出生了，我们也会给你洗香香的泡泡浴。你会像哼哼一样，发现原来洗澡，是一件这么好玩、这么温暖的事。

✨ 故事小语
有些事看着不情愿，试过才知道有多好玩。洗澡呀，就是把一天的脏脏，都变成香香的泡泡。

Piglet's Bubble Bath (Prenatal)

Piglet Hengheng hates bath time. The moment bath time comes, he grunts and wiggles under the bed: I don't want a bath! I don't want a bath!

But today, mama fills the tub with lots and lots of bubbles. White, soft bubbles pile up like a little mountain, and they smell sweet, like oranges.

Hengheng peeks out half his head, and his eyes light up. He climbs in — gurgle, gurgle — and the bubbles rush up — splash, splash — wrapping him into a round, bubbly pig.

He pokes his snout into a puff of bubbles and blows — poof! The bubbles float up and drift around the bathroom. Mama watches and giggles.

Little one, can you hear it? That is papa laughing by the bathroom door, low and warm like distant thunder, a sound that makes everything feel safe.

After the bath, Hengheng is wrapped tight in a big towel — sweet-smelling and soft. He suddenly thinks: a bath isn't so bad after all.

Little one, when you are born, we will give you a sweet bubble bath too. You will find, like Hengheng, that bath time is such a warm, happy thing.

✨ 故事小语
Some things seem unpleasant until you try them. A bath turns a day's grime into sweet-smelling bubbles.
