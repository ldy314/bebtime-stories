小狐狸的第一场雪（胎教期）

森林深处，住着一只火红的小狐狸。它听老松树说，等天再冷一点，天上会落下来一种白白软软的东西，叫「雪」。小狐狸从来没见过雪，它等啊等，把耳朵竖得尖尖的。

夜里，风呼呼地刮过树梢，小狐狸缩在洞口，把尾巴盖在鼻尖上。它听见远处有小溪汩汩地流，有猫头鹰咕——咕——地叫，可雪还是没有来。

它有点困了，眼皮一下一下地往下掉。就在它快要睡着的时候，天上忽然飘下一片、两片，轻轻的小白点，像谁撒下了一把会飞的羽毛。

小狐狸一下子蹦起来，追着雪花跑。雪花落在它黑黑的鼻尖上，凉丝丝的；落在它火红的尾巴上，一闪就化了。整个森林，都变成了白茫茫、软绵绵的一片。

小宝宝，你感觉到了吗？这世界上有那么多第一次，都像这场雪一样，正安安静静地等着你。圆圆的月亮，像一盏小夜灯，也陪着小狐狸，等着你呢。

小狐狸在雪地里打了个滚，留下了一串小小的脚印。它想：等你来了，一定要带你来看第一场雪，看脚印在雪地上排成一条回家的路。

宝宝呀，慢慢等，慢慢长。这世界最美的风景，都会为耐心等待的你，一场一场地准备好。

✨ 故事小语
最美的风景，总是为耐心等待的人准备。你还没来，这个世界就已经为你留好了许多第一次。

The Little Fox's First Snow (Prenatal)

Deep in the forest lives a red little fox. The old pine tree tells him that when it grows colder, something white and soft called snow will fall from the sky. The little fox has never seen snow. He waits, ears pricked up tall.

At night, the wind blows — whoosh, whoosh — through the treetops. The little fox curls in his den, his tail over his nose. He hears a stream gurgling far away, and an owl hooting — hoo, hoo — but the snow has not come.

He grows sleepy, his eyes drooping shut. Just as he is about to fall asleep, the sky lets down one, then two, soft little white dots, like someone scattering flying feathers.

The little fox leaps up and chases the snowflakes. One lands on his black nose — cool and tingly. One lands on his red tail and melts in a flash. The whole forest turns white and soft and quiet.

Little one, can you feel it? So many firsts in this world are waiting quietly for you, just like this snow. The round moon, like a little night-light, keeps the little fox company, and waits for you too.

The little fox rolls in the snow, leaving a trail of tiny paw prints. He thinks: when you come, I will bring you to see the first snow, and watch our footprints line up into a road that leads home.

Little one, wait slowly, grow slowly. This world's most beautiful sights will be prepared for you, one by one, if you wait with patience.

✨ 故事小语
The most beautiful sights are prepared for those who wait patiently. Before you even arrive, this world has kept many firsts for you.
