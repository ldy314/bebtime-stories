妈妈的摇篮曲（胎教期）

月亮婆婆轻轻晃，星星宝宝眨眼睛。妈妈的手放在肚子上，唱起软软的摇篮曲。

小宝宝在妈妈肚子里，像一条小鱼游啊游，游进温暖的梦里。

妈妈唱：'睡吧，睡吧，我亲爱的宝贝，妈妈的怀抱，是甜甜的港湾。'

宝宝听见了，小脚丫轻轻踢一踢，好像在说：'妈妈，我在这里呀。'

爸爸也来了，他贴着妈妈的肚子，小声说：'小宝贝，我是爸爸，等你长大，我们一起去数星星。'

宝宝听见了，小手手挥一挥，好像在说：'爸爸，我记住啦。'

风儿轻轻吹，窗帘慢慢摇，妈妈的声音像羽毛，痒痒的，暖暖的。

宝宝在肚子里，翻了个身，蜷成一个小圆球，像一颗小种子，躲在泥土里，等着春天发芽。

妈妈闭上眼，心里轻轻说：'宝贝，我们等你，等了好久好久。'

月光洒进窗子，照在妈妈的笑脸上，宝宝在梦里，也笑了。

整个世界都安静了，只有妈妈的心跳，扑通，扑通，像一首最古老的歌。

宝宝听着听着，睡得香香的，在妈妈的爱里，慢慢长大。

✨ 故事小语
宝宝在妈妈肚子里，听着妈妈的心跳和歌声，感受到满满的爱与等待。

The Little Star's Treasure Hunt (Prenatal)

Softly, softly, a little star twinkled high above the sleepy world. It whispered to the moon, 'I want to find a treasure, a special one, before the night is through.' The moon smiled a gentle silver smile and said, 'Little star, the treasure you seek is not far away. Follow the quiet breeze that hums a lullaby, and let it guide you on your way.'

So the little star drifted down, down, down, through the velvet sky, past clouds as soft as sheep's wool, floating slowly, gently by. The breeze sang a soft 'shhhh, shhhh' as it danced among the leaves. 'Follow me,' it seemed to say, 'to a secret place of ease.'

Down in a cozy meadow, where the grass was soft and deep, a mother rabbit hummed a tune to her babies, fast asleep. 'Shhh,' she said, 'my little ones, the night is calm and still. The stars are watching over us, and all the world is still.' The little star peeked through the grass and saw the baby bunnies snuggle, their noses twitching, ears so soft, all warm inside their huddle.

The breeze then tickled a sleepy stream that sang a 'gurgle, gurgle, plip.' 'Come this way,' the water whispered, 'I know a treasure fit.' The star followed, past a sleeping owl, who hooted a soft 'too-whoo.' 'The treasure's nearly found now, just a little further for you.'

At last the breeze grew very still, and the star saw a sight so dear: a little bird in a nest so warm, with a tiny golden tear. 'Oh, hello there,' the birdie chirped, 'I lost my shiny bead. It rolled away into the dark, when I had so much need.' The star twinkled bright and said, 'Don't cry, I'll help you find it, you and I.'

They searched beneath a mushroom cap, and under a leafy bed. The star glowed softly, 'There! I see it,' as the bead shone up ahead. The little bird gave a happy 'tweet,' and hopped with joy to see. 'You found my treasure, lovely star! Now will you stay with me?'

The star smiled down and said, 'My treasure was to help you, dear. For the greatest treasure in the world is to bring a friend a cheer.' The bird and star then sang a song, a soft and sleepy tune, and the star floated up, up, up, back to the silver moon.

And as the star settled in the sky, it looked down at the nest so sweet. The bird was sleeping, bead in wing, with a happy little beat. The star whispered to the moon, 'I found my treasure, true.' And the moon replied, 'Yes, little one, the treasure was you.'

So close your eyes, little one, and listen to the breeze. The world is full of gentle joys, and you are loved with ease. Shhh, shhh, the night is soft, the stars are twinkling high. Sleep tight, my dear, and dream of treasure, beneath the quiet sky.

✨ 故事小语
The greatest treasure is the love we carry and share with others.
