一朵云的小睡（胎教期）

天上有一朵小小的云，白白的，软软的，像一床刚晒过的棉被。它从东边飘到西边，飘呀飘，忽然觉得有点困了。

它想找一个地方睡午觉。可是天空那么大，风又一直推着它走。小云打了个哈欠，身子软软地往下沉了一点。

一棵大树的树梢，正好托住了它。树上的知了放轻了声音，吱——地叫了一半就停了。太阳也把光调得柔柔的。

小云趴在树梢上，听着树叶沙沙沙，听着远处溪水叮叮咚咚，像一首没有词的摇篮曲。它一点点闭上眼睛。

小宝宝，你感觉到了吗？这世界就像一双看不见的手，正轻轻托着你，像大树托着小云一样。星星们手拉手，围成一个小小的摇篮，轻轻摇着你。

小云睡了一个甜甜的午觉。醒来的时候，天边有一抹淡淡的金，它觉得自己轻得像一片羽毛，又能飘啦。

宝宝呀，累的时候就歇一歇。等你准备好了，外面的世界有软软的风，有圆圆的月亮，还有好多好多安静又温柔的时光，等着你。

✨ 故事小语
累了的时候，就停下来歇一歇，世界会轻轻接住你。安安静静的时光，也是甜甜的。

🔬 🔬科学故事（胎教期）：露珠是怎么来的

清晨，小草尖上挂着一颗亮晶晶的露珠，圆圆的，像一颗透明的珍珠。宝宝，你以后也会在清晨的草叶上看见它。可是你知道吗？露珠不是天上掉下来的雨。

空气里呀，藏着许多看不见的水汽，像一群透明的小精灵，飘来飘去。白天太阳暖暖地照着，这些小精灵热乎乎的，散在空气里，谁也看不见。

到了夜里，天变凉了。空气像一双慢慢变冷的手，握不住那么多水汽啦。那些凉了的小水汽，就手拉手，凝在冰凉凉的草叶上、花瓣上，变成一颗颗小水珠。这就是露珠。

太阳一出来，暖暖的光照着露珠，露珠又变成了看不见的水汽，轻飘飘地飞回天空。所以呀，露珠只在清晨出现，太阳一高，它们就悄悄回家了。

宝宝，你听，露珠滴答一声从叶尖滑落，落到泥土里，叫醒了一颗刚发芽的种子。水就是这样，一会儿在天空，一会儿在地上，做着温柔的旅行。

等你来到这个世界，某个凉爽的清晨，也可以和爸爸妈妈一起去草地上看看：那些亮晶晶的小水珠，就是夜里空气送给小草的礼物。而你呀，也像一颗刚凝成的小露珠，正被爸爸妈妈的爱，温柔地捧着。

✨ 故事小语
空气里藏着看不见的水汽，夜里凉下来，水汽就结成露珠；太阳一晒，露珠又悄悄回到空气里。水就这样在地面和天空之间，做着温柔的旅行。

A Little Cloud's Nap (Prenatal)

High in the sky floats a small white cloud, soft as a quilt fresh from the sun. It drifts from east to west, and after a while, it feels a little sleepy.

It wants to find a place for a nap. But the sky is so wide, and the wind keeps pushing it along. The little cloud yawns and sinks softly down.

A tall tree's treetop catches it. The cicada lowers its voice — zee — and stops halfway. The sun softens its light too.

The little cloud nestles on the treetop, listening to the leaves — rustle, rustle — and the faraway stream — plip-plop. It is a lullaby without words. Its eyes slowly close.

Little one, can you feel it? The world is like a pair of invisible hands, holding you up, the way the tree holds the little cloud. The stars join hands into a tiny cradle and rock you gently.

The little cloud wakes from a sweet nap. A faint gold glows at the sky's edge, and it feels light as a feather, ready to float again.

Little one, rest when you are tired. When you are ready, the soft wind and the round moon will be waiting, along with so many quiet, gentle hours.

✨ 故事小语
When you are tired, stop and rest — the world will catch you gently. Quiet time is sweet time too.

🔬 🔬 Science Story (Prenatal): Where Morning Dew Comes From

In the early morning, a bright dewdrop rests on a blade of grass — round and clear, like a little pearl of glass. Little one, one day you will see it too. But do you know? Dew does not fall as rain.

The air is full of invisible water vapor, like a crowd of tiny see-through sprites drifting everywhere. In the warm daylight, they float loosely, and no one can see them.

At night, the air cools, like hands slowly turning cold that can no longer hold so much vapor. The cooled vapor links hands and gathers on the cool grass and petals, turning into little drops. That is dew.

When the sun rises, its warm light touches the dew, and the drops turn back into invisible vapor, floating up to the sky. So dew appears only in the early morning — when the sun climbs high, it quietly goes home.

Little one, listen: drip — a dewdrop slides off a leaf and falls to the soil, waking a seed just sprouting. This is how water travels, sometimes in the sky, sometimes on the ground, a gentle journey.

When you arrive, on a cool morning, you can go with mama and papa to the grass: those tiny bright drops are the air's gift to the grass at night. And you, little one, are like a newly gathered dewdrop, held tenderly in mama and papa's love.

✨ 故事小语
The air holds invisible water vapor; when the night cools, the vapor becomes dew, and when the sun warms it, the dew slips back into the air. Water travels gently between earth and sky.
