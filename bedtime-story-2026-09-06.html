云朵精灵朵朵的月光远行（胎教期）

傍晚，云朵精灵朵朵坐在云端棉田上，抖了抖蓬松的云朵口袋。忽然，风从耳边呼呼吹过，带来一缕细细的声音：「谁帮我给远方的小岛捎一封温暖的信？」朵朵竖起耳朵：「我来！我飘呀飘，最爱替大家送温暖了。」

朵朵把信卷成一片小小的云，揣进口袋，顺着风出发了。它飘过金色的麦田，麦浪哗啦哗啦地弯腰，像在向它问好。它飘过森林，树梢沙沙沙地响，叶子们挥着小手说：「一路顺风呀，朵朵。」

月亮升起来了，圆圆的，像一盏挂在天空的小夜灯。朵朵飘进一片温柔的月光里，月光把它照得透亮透亮的，像一块软软的果冻。「月光也跟我一起远行呢，」朵朵想，「有了月亮陪着，多远的路都不孤单。」

天亮之前，朵朵终于飘到了那座小岛。岛上有一棵老树，正望着海面发呆。朵朵把云做的信轻轻放进老树的枝丫里：「这是远方的朋友托我捎来的温暖。」老树抖了抖叶子，树梢上竟慢慢亮起一朵朵小花。「谢谢你，」老树说，「原来还有人想着我呀。」

朵朵没有立刻回家。它陪老树看了海上的日出，听海鸥叽叽喳喳地聊天，又给岛上几株口渴的小草下了一场小小的、温柔的雨。哗啦哗啦，雨点轻轻落下来，小草们仰着头，咕嘟咕嘟喝了个饱。

第二天傍晚，朵朵要回家了。老树摘下一片最亮的叶子送它：「这是我送你的温暖，带回给你的朋友们吧。」朵朵把叶子放进云朵口袋，顺着晚风飘呀飘，一路把温暖分给遇到的每一朵云、每一只鸟、每一缕光。

回到云端棉田的时候，月亮又圆又亮，像月光守候着整个天空。朵朵把老树的叶子轻轻放在枕边，想着：这一趟远行，我送出了温暖，也收回了满满一袋子的温柔。小宝宝，等你准备好了，这世界的风、月光和老树，都会为你准备同样的温柔。现在，先安心长大，好梦。

✨ 故事小语
把温柔送给远方，再带着满满的温柔回家，是一件顶好的事。

Bear Safe and the Four Good Nights (Prenatal)

Bear Safe lived in a forest hollow, soft and slow and full of reassurance. "Huff... lu..." it sighed, as it always did, stretching its big warm paws. This week, Bear Safe decided: "I will visit four friends, and give each one a good night."

On Monday, Bear Safe padded to the stream. The water went fizz, fizz over the stones. "Little stream," said Bear, "you sing all day and all night. Here is a good night for you." And it scooped a warm pawful of moonlight and poured it into the stream, which glowed and sang a little softer. Thump, thump went Bear's heart, happy and slow.

On Tuesday, Bear Safe visited the big oak. "Old tree," it said, "you hold up the whole sky. Here is a good night for you." And it leaned its warm back against the trunk and hummed a low, sleepy song, la-la-la, until the tree's branches relaxed and its leaves stopped shivering.

On Wednesday, Bear Safe visited the fireflies. "Little lanterns," it said, "you carry the dark for everyone. Here is a good night for you." And it blew them each a soft warm breath, and the fireflies blinked their little lights in answer — one, two, three, good night.

On Thursday, Bear Safe climbed to the hilltop and called to the moon. "Round moon," it said, "you watch over the whole forest. Here is a good night for you." And the moon smiled its quiet silver smile, and a ring of warm light like open arms held Bear Safe steady, as if in a soft little nest.

Then Bear Safe padded home to its hollow and curled into a warm ball. "Huff... lu..." it sighed. "Giving good nights is the nicest thing. Now I will sleep, and tomorrow I will give good mornings."

Little one in Mommy's tummy, while you grow safe and sound, the world is giving you a good night too: the stream, the oak, the fireflies, the moon, and everyone who loves you. Sleep in a warm little nest of love. Huff... lu... good night, little one.

✨ 故事小语
A good night is love in its smallest, warmest form — and it comes back to you.
