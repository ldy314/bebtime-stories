蒲公英的远行（胎教期）

草地上，开着一朵蒲公英。它的头顶，是一颗颗白色的小伞，挤在一起，像一团软软的、会发光的云。风一吹，沙沙沙，小伞们就轻轻地晃。

有一天，风来了。风轻轻地说：走吧，孩子们，我带你们去看外面的世界。小伞们沙沙沙地点头，一朵接一朵，呼——地离开了妈妈的花茎。

它们飘呀飘，飞过了金黄的麦田，飞过了亮晶晶的小河，飞过了正在打盹的小山坡。每一把小伞，都像一艘小小的、透明的船，摇啊摇。

天快黑了。有一把小伞，落在一片安静的草地上，轻轻地说：就在这里吧。它钻进软软的泥土里，盖上一床薄薄的土被子，甜甜地睡着了。

小宝宝，你听见了吗？风里传来一阵温柔的哼歌声，像云朵被子一样，轻轻裹着那颗睡着了的小种子。妈妈也会这样，一路陪着你。

春天来的时候，那颗小种子会发芽，会长成新的蒲公英，再开出许多白色的小伞。到那时，它也会让风，把自己的孩子送去远方。

宝宝呀，你也是一颗正在妈妈肚子里安睡的小种子。等时候到了，你也会在爱里，找到属于你的地方，生根，发芽，开出自己的花。

✨ 故事小语
离开是为了更好的相遇。每一颗小种子，都会在风里找到自己生根发芽的地方。

The Dandelion's Journey (Prenatal)

On the meadow grows a dandelion, crowned with tiny white parachutes, crowded together like a soft, glowing cloud.

One day the wind comes and says gently: come, little ones, I will show you the wide world. The parachutes nod — rustle, rustle — and one by one, they let go of their mother's stem.

They drift and float, over golden wheat, over a sparkling stream, over a dozing hillside. Each little parachute is like a tiny, see-through boat.

As evening falls, one parachute settles on a quiet patch of grass and says softly: here, this is the place. It tucks into the soft soil, pulls a thin blanket of earth over itself, and sleeps sweetly.

Little one, can you hear it? A gentle humming drifts in the wind, like a cloud blanket, wrapping the sleeping seed. Mama will stay with you, all along the way, just the same.

When spring comes, that seed will sprout, and grow into a new dandelion, opening many little white parachutes. Then it, too, will send its children off into the wind.

Little one, you are a small seed sleeping inside mama. When the time comes, you will take root in love, sprout, and bloom a flower all your own.

✨ 故事小语
Leaving is for a better meeting. Every little seed finds, in the wind, a place of its own to take root and grow.
