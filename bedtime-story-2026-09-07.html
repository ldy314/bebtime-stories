小熊安安的软软一天（胎教期）

小熊安安从树洞里醒来，呼……噜……，伸了一个大大的懒腰。阳光从洞口漏进来，暖融融的。安安看了看自己的手掌，又看了看自己的小脚，决定今天要好好用它们感受这一天。小宝宝，你也想试试吗？用小手掌和小脚丫，去摸一摸、踩一踩这个世界。

安安走到小河边，把软软的小脚伸进水里。水是凉凉的、滑滑的，汩汩地从脚趾间流过，像一群调皮的小鱼在挠痒痒。「嘻嘻，」安安笑了，「水在跟我玩游戏呢。」它又蹲下来，用手掌轻轻拍水，啪嗒啪嗒，溅起一串亮晶晶的小水花。

安安走到森林里，发现一棵大树倒下的地方长满了青苔。它把手掌轻轻贴上去——毛茸茸的、软软的，像一块绿色的小地毯。「原来森林也有软软的地毯呀。」安安趴下来，把脸也贴上去，深深地吸了一口带着泥土香气的空气。

午后的风呼呼地吹过，树梢淅淅沥沥地落下一些水珠——是昨夜雨留下的。水珠落在安安的鼻尖上，凉凉的、圆圆的。安安舔了舔嘴唇：「雨水是甜的吗？」风里传来沙沙的回应，好像树们在笑：小傻瓜，雨水的味道要自己尝才知道。

傍晚，安安回到树洞口，把今天的感觉一样一样数给月亮听：水是凉凉滑滑的，青苔是毛茸茸软软的，雨滴是凉凉甜甜的。月亮微笑着，把银色的光轻轻铺在安安身上。这时，远处传来爸爸低沉温柔的笑声，像春天的雷声一样让人安心。

安安竖着耳朵听了一会儿，心里暖洋洋的。「爸爸的笑声，是今天最软的东西呀。」它钻回树洞，把小爪子蜷起来，把自己团成一个圆圆的、暖暖的小球。「呼……噜……」安安轻轻睡着了，嘴角还挂着笑。

小宝宝，等你准备好了，你也可以像小熊安安一样，用小手小脚去感受这个世界：凉凉的水、软软的青苔、甜甜的雨，还有家人温暖的笑声。它们都在等你。现在，先在妈妈怀里，把日子过得软软的、暖暖的。好梦，小宝宝。

✨ 故事小语
慢慢走，慢慢摸，慢慢听，把每一刻都过得软软的、暖暖的，就是好时光。

Moon Mama's Lullaby Choir (Prenatal)

Moon Mama lived in the night sky, gentle and protective and full of wisdom. "Sleep safe, Mama is here," she would whisper to every sleeping creature. One night, Moon Mama had an idea. "Tonight," she said, "I will gather the whole world into a choir of good-night sounds."

She began with the little house on the hill. "Little clock," she said, "sing your softest note." Ding-dong went the clock, once, gently, like a yawn. "Good," said Moon Mama. "Now you, little dog." The dog gave one small, sleepy woof, then tucked its nose under its tail.

Moon Mama floated to the garden. "Little bees," she said, "your work is done. Sing your rest song." Buzz went the bees, soft and low, drowsy as honey. The flowers nodded their heads and closed their petals, one by one. Pitter went the rain, starting so softly it was almost a whisper, pattering the leaves like tiny fingers on a drum.

Moon Mama called to the wind. "Wind," she said, "sing through the trees." Whoosh went the wind, gentle and slow, and the leaves answered, shhhh, shhhh, like a thousand soft voices hushing the night. "Now you, river," she said, and the river went gurgle, gurgle, a low, rocking song.

Then Moon Mama turned to the town and sang the last line herself, in her silver, motherly voice. And all the sounds rose together — the ding-dong, the woof, the buzz, the pitter, the whoosh, the gurgle — weaving into one great lullaby that wrapped around the whole sleeping world like a soft blanket.

"Little one in Mommy's tummy," whispered Moon Mama, and a round moon like a little night-light quietly kept the baby company, softening every shadow. "Can you hear the world singing? It is all for you. Ding-dong, buzz, pitter, whoosh — every sound is someone saying: we are here, sleep safe."

Little one, when you are ready, you will find the world is a choir, and every voice in it loves you: the clock, the dog, the bees, the rain, the wind, the river, and the people who wait for you with open arms. Sleep safe, Mama is here. Good night, little one.

✨ 故事小语
When everyone sings softly together, the world becomes one great lullaby.
