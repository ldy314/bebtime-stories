月光摇篮曲（胎教期）

啾啾，睡吧。屋檐下的小鸟啾啾，望着圆圆的月亮，想为肚子里的小宝宝唱一首歌。它轻轻抖了抖羽毛，发出沙沙的响声，像风儿在树叶间散步。

啾啾想：小宝宝在妈妈的肚子里，一定像躺在云朵做的摇篮里，轻轻摇啊摇。

于是，啾啾展开翅膀，飞过亮晶晶的星星，来到月光海面。海浪轻轻地拍着，哗啦哗啦，像妈妈的心跳，咚咚，咚咚。啾啾把月光装进羽毛里，羽毛变得软软的，暖暖的。

它继续飞，飞过云端棉田，那里有白白的云朵，像棉花糖一样。啾啾用喙轻轻啄了一小片，云朵发出噗噗的声音，像枕头在呼吸。

这时，一阵温柔的哼歌声从远处传来，像云朵被子一样，把啾啾轻轻裹住。哼歌里藏着摇篮曲的节拍，摇啊摇，摇啊摇。啾啾闭上眼睛，感觉自己也变成了小宝宝，被妈妈抱在怀里。

小宝宝，你听到了吗？那是妈妈的哼歌声，像温暖的毛线团，一圈一圈，把你包在中间。你感觉到吗？月光像星星灯笼，照亮你小小的梦。

啾啾带着羽毛和云朵，飞回屋檐下，它把月光和哼歌声，编成一首摇篮曲，藏在窝里。等你准备好了，外面的世界有软软的风和圆圆的月亮，等你。

晚安，小宝宝，月亮陪着你，妈妈的心跳陪你。啾啾，睡吧。

✨ 故事小语
爱与守护，就像月光一样温柔地包裹着每一个小生命，等待他安心长大。

The Little Pot's Good-Night Glow (Prenatal)

In a cozy corner of the kitchen, a tiny potted plant sat on the windowsill, its leaves swaying soft as a lullaby. Every night, when the moon rose round and low, the little pot would whisper to the baby in Mama's belly, 'Float, float, home at last.'

The little pot had a secret wish: to give the baby a good-night glow, a warm little light to say 'I'm here, growing with you.' So it stretched its leaves toward the moon, catching silver beams and dewy drops, and began to hum a tiny tune—hum-hum, hum-hum, like a sleepy bee.

A breeze blew through the window, soft and slow, and carried the little pot's song up, up, into the night. The song swirled around the room, wrapping Mama's belly in a gentle bubble of warmth, like a cloud cradling a star. Can you feel it, little one? The soft cloud walls, the cozy sway—the world is holding you close.

The little pot's leaves turned a soft, golden green, glowing like a tiny lantern. It painted the room with a gentle light, warm as honey, soft as fur. The moonlight outside peeked in, and the stars winked, and the pot's glow grew brighter, warmer, like a blanket of light wrapping you tight.

Then, through the glow, the little pot heard a sound—thump, thump, thump. It was Mama's heartbeat, steady and strong, like a drum calling you home. The little pot smiled and whispered, 'Little one, can you hear it? That's Mama's heart, singing you a lullaby of love.'

The glow wrapped around the heartbeat, and the heartbeat wrapped around you, and the little pot's leaves swayed, swayed, swayed. The room was filled with the smell of fresh earth and the taste of sweet dreams, and the pot's light was a cradle of stars, rocking you gently.

And the little pot said, 'Float, float, home at last. When you are ready, the soft wind and round moon will be waiting, and Mama's heart will be your pillow, warm and true. Good night, little one, the moon is with you.'

✨ 故事小语
Even the smallest, quietest things hold a world of love, and waiting for you is the gentlest song of all.
