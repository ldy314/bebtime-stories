【胎教期】月亮妈妈的摇篮曲

月亮妈妈挂在天空上，圆圆的，亮亮的，像一个大大的气球。她轻轻地哼着歌：「唔——唔——小宝宝，快快睡，妈妈在，不怕黑。」

风娃娃听见了，悄悄地吹过来，把歌声送到小树上。小树的叶子「沙沙沙」地响，好像在说：「真好听，真好听。」

月亮妈妈又哼着歌：「唔——唔——小星星，眨眼睛，妈妈的爱，亮晶晶。」星星们听见了，一闪一闪地眨着眼睛，好像在跳舞。

这时候，妈妈肚子里的小宝宝也听见了。他翻了个身，耳朵贴着小房子，听着月亮妈妈的歌。他的心「扑通扑通」跳着，像在打拍子。

妈妈把手放在肚子上，轻轻地说：「宝宝，你听，月亮妈妈在唱歌呢。她在等春天，等花开。妈妈也在等你，等你睁开眼睛，看看这个美丽的世界。」

月亮妈妈笑了，她的光变得更柔、更暖了，像一条软软的毯子，盖在大地上。宝宝打了个哈欠，闭上眼睛，在妈妈的歌声里，慢慢地睡着了。

后来啊，月亮妈妈每天都来唱歌。她唱呀唱，直到春天来了，花儿开了，宝宝也出生了。宝宝一听到妈妈的声音，就不哭了，因为他知道——那是世界上最温暖的声音。

✨ 故事小语
妈妈的声音是世界上最好听的声音，它像月光一样温柔，守护着宝宝长大。等待，也是爱的一部分。

The Little Lantern's Journey (Prenatal)

In a quiet little town where the moon rose like a golden balloon, there lived a tiny lantern named Lumi. Lumi was no ordinary lantern—he was born from a firefly's wish and a mother's soft hum. Every night, he sat on a windowsill, glowing gently, waiting for someone to need him.

One evening, a little breeze whispered, 「Lumi, Lumi, a baby cloud has lost her way. She's floating all alone behind the hills. Can you help her find home?」 Lumi blinked his warm, golden light. 「I will try,」 he said softly, and off he drifted into the twilight.

He floated past a sleepy owl who hooted, 「Hoo, hoo, where are you going, little glow?」 Lumi answered, 「To find a lost cloud and bring her home.」 The owl nodded and said, 「Follow the sound of the lullaby—the river sings it every night.」

Lumi drifted down, down, down, to where the river hummed a gentle tune: 「Shhh, shhh, shhh.」 He saw a tiny white cloud huddled near a willow tree, shivering. 「Don't be scared,」 Lumi said. 「I'll guide you back. Just follow my light.」

Together they floated over fields of sleeping flowers—daisies dreaming of sunshine, tulips nodding in the breeze. The little cloud began to feel braver. 「Can you make your light blink like a star?」 she asked. Lumi blinked three times: blink, blink, blink. The cloud giggled, a soft pitter-patter of rain.

Soon they reached the sky's edge, where the moon hung low and golden. A big, puffy Mama Cloud was waiting with open arms. 「My little one!」 she said, wrapping the baby cloud in a hug. Then she turned to Lumi. 「Thank you, tiny lantern. Without you, she might have drifted forever.」

Lumi smiled and said, 「I just followed the lullaby. And the owl. And the river.」 Mama Cloud gave him a kiss of dew, and Lumi felt warm all over. He floated back to his windowsill, where the moon was now high and bright.

As he settled, a voice came from inside the house—a mother's voice, soft and low, humming the same lullaby the river sang. Lumi glowed a little brighter, knowing he was home. And he knew: even the smallest light can guide a lost heart home.

Goodnight, little Lumi. Goodnight, little one. May you always find your way home, just like the baby cloud. Sleep tight, and dream of gentle lights and loving voices.

✨ 故事小语
Even a tiny light can find its way home with love, and every journey is easier when you listen to a loving voice.
