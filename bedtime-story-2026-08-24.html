小刺猬的苹果（胎教期）

秋天到了，树林里落满了金黄的叶子。小刺猬在树下发现了一个又大又红的苹果，香喷喷的，亮晶晶的。

它想把苹果背回家，可苹果圆溜溜的，它用爪子推，苹果骨碌碌滚走了；它用背拱，苹果又咕噜一下滑下来。

小刺猬有点着急了。它坐在树下，呼哧呼哧地喘着气。一只小鸟啾啾地飞过来，说：别急呀，想想你身上有什么宝贝？

小刺猬低头一看，呀，自己背上长满了细细的小刺。它把身子往苹果上一靠，轻轻一拱——苹果稳稳地扎在了刺上，再也滚不掉了。

小宝宝，你感觉到了吗？一床云朵一样软的小被子，正轻轻盖过来，把你裹成一个小小的、暖暖的茧。你呀，也有一身属于自己的小宝贝，等着慢慢发现。

小刺猬背着苹果，高高兴兴地往家走。一路上，树叶沙沙沙地为它鼓掌，小蘑菇都探出脑袋来看它。

宝宝呀，等你来到这个世界，也会遇到圆溜溜的小难题。别着急，慢慢想，你身上一定藏着解决它的小本领。

✨ 故事小语
遇到困难不着急，慢慢想办法，总会找到适合自己的方式。就像小刺猬，用身上的刺，背起了最爱的苹果。

🔬 🔬科学故事（胎教期）：大雁为什么要排队飞

秋天的天空，大雁排成一个大大的「人」字往南飞。宝宝，你以后也会在秋天看见它们。可是你知道，它们为什么要排着队飞吗？

大雁要飞很远很远的路，从北方的家，飞到温暖的南方去过冬。这一路，要飞过田野、飞过大河、飞过高山，谁都会累。

于是它们想出了一个好办法：排成队飞。最前面的大雁用力扇翅膀，就会在身后带起一股向上吹的风。后面的伙伴借着这股风，就像被轻轻托着，飞起来省力多了。

排头的大雁最累。所以呀，飞一会儿，它就会退到后面休息，换另一只大雁到前面去领头。大家轮流当队长，谁也不一直累着。

宝宝，你听，「嘎——嘎——」，那是大雁们在互相打气呢。它们叫一叫，就是告诉伙伴：加油呀，快到了。这一声声，比风还暖。

等飞到南方，天也暖和了，湖水也软了。大雁们落在水边，咕咕地歇着，等着下一个春天，再一起飞回北方的家。

宝宝呀，大雁的故事告诉我们：一个人会累，大家一起，互相帮着，就能飞得更远。等你出生了，我们一家人，也会像大雁一样，排着队，护着你往前走。

✨ 故事小语
大雁排成「人」字或「一」字飞，前面大雁扇动翅膀会带起一股向上的风，让后面的伙伴飞起来更省力；领头累了就换一只，大家轮流当队长，才能飞过千山万水。

The Little Hedgehog's Apple (Prenatal)

Autumn has come, and the woods are covered in golden leaves. Under a tree, the little hedgehog finds a big, red apple — sweet-smelling and shiny.

He wants to carry it home, but the apple is so round. He pushes it with his paws, and it rolls away — roll, roll. He nudges it with his back, and it slips off — plop.

The little hedgehog grows a little worried. He sits under the tree, puffing — huff, huff. A little bird flits down — tweet, tweet — and says: don't worry, what treasure do you have on your back?

The hedgehog looks down. Oh! His back is covered with fine little quills. He leans against the apple and gives a little nudge — and the apple sticks firmly to his quills, never rolling away again.

Little one, can you feel it? A cloud-soft blanket is tucking over you, wrapping you into a small, warm cocoon. You, too, have little treasures of your own, waiting to be discovered.

The little hedgehog carries the apple home, happy as can be. Along the way, the leaves rustle, clapping for him, and the little mushrooms poke their heads out to watch.

Little one, when you arrive, you will meet round little problems too. Don't rush — think slowly, and you will find the little skill hidden in you to solve them.

✨ 故事小语
When you meet a hard problem, do not rush — think slowly and you will find your own way. Like the hedgehog, who used his quills to carry his favorite apple.

🔬 🔬 Science Story (Prenatal): Why Geese Fly in a V

In the autumn sky, geese fly south in a great letter V. Little one, one day you will see them too. But do you know why they fly in a line?

Geese travel a very long way, from their northern home to the warm south for winter. Along the way they cross fields, rivers, and mountains — and everyone gets tired.

So they found a clever way: fly in a line. The lead goose flaps hard, and its wings lift the air behind it, like a gentle updraft. The ones behind ride this wind, as if held up softly, and fly with far less effort.

The lead goose works hardest. So after a while it drops back to rest, and another goose flies to the front. They take turns being leader, so no one stays tired for long.

Little one, listen — honk, honk — that is the geese cheering each other on. Each call says: keep going, we are almost there. It is warmer than the wind.

When they reach the south, the weather is warm and the water soft. The geese settle by the water, resting, waiting for the next spring to fly home together.

Little one, the geese teach us: one alone grows tired, but together, helping each other, we fly farther. When you are born, our family will fly in line like the geese, shielding you as we go.

✨ 故事小语
Geese fly in a V or a line because the lead goose's flapping wings lift the air behind, making it easier for the ones following; when the leader tires, another takes its place, so everyone takes turns and the whole flock can cross mountains and rivers.
