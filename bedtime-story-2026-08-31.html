小海螺小旋旋的光之旅行（胎教期）

深海的海草森林里，小海螺小旋旋睁着亮亮的小眼睛，对什么都好奇。今晚，它想看看光是怎么玩耍的。

呼呼，水流推着它慢慢飘；咕嘟咕嘟，气泡像一串小灯笼从脚边升起来。小旋旋跟着光斑，从海草的缝里钻了出去。

淅淅沥沥，月光洒在水面上，被波浪揉成了会跳舞的小影子。墙上映出摇摇摆摆的光，像在和它捉迷藏。小旋旋看呆了：原来光也会玩游戏呀。

远远地，传来爸爸低沉温柔的笑声，像春天的雷声一样让人安心。小旋旋把笑声装进贝壳里，想留着给还没出生的小宝宝听。

原来是这样呀……小旋旋轻轻叹了口气。它忽然明白，光的游戏背后，是整片温柔的世界在陪着它。

晚安，小宝宝，月亮陪着你。等你准备好了，小旋旋带你，去看会跳舞的光，和爸爸藏在风里的笑声。

✨ 故事小语
世界很大，奇妙很多，但你永远被温柔的光和笑声包围着，慢慢探索就好。

Little Bird's Aurora Song (Prenatal)

Under the eaves lived a little bird named Chirp who sang lullabies, gentle and sweet. Tweet, tweet, sleep now, she would say to the round little tummy below.

One night Chirp flew up to see the dance of the aurora — ruff went her wings, and wiggle went the green and violet ribbons across the sky. It was the world's own lullaby, painted high.

Ding-dong, a far ice crystal rang, clear as a bell. Chirp looked down at Lake Baikal's clarity, where the water held the stars like a second sky, and at the Sahara's starry hush, where the sand kept every ancient secret.

She remembered the earth's slow rotation, turning all these wonders past the baby's unseen window. Moonlight watched: a round moon like a little night-light, quietly keeping the baby company, softening every shadow.

Chirp folded a scrap of aurora into her song and dropped it, soft as a feather, onto Mama's tummy. Little one, can you hear the colors? They are singing you a goodnight.

Good night, little one. The whole wide world is rehearsing its welcome — and the moon is right there, smiling, just for you.

✨ 故事小语
The world is full of quiet wonders waiting for you — and you are watched over by love, even before you arrive.
