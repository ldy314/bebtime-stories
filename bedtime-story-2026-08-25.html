月亮妈妈的哼歌（胎教期）

夜空里，月亮妈妈轻轻守着一团暖暖的小光，那光里住着还没有出生的小宝宝。她弯下腰，轻声问：小宝贝，你猜「我是谁」呀？

风从窗边溜过来，把夜晚的声音都染上了颜色——蛐蛐蛐蛐，是绿色的小提琴；远处的钟摆咯噔咯噔，是金色的摇椅在慢慢摇。

月亮妈妈飘过人家的窗台，看见会发光的夜灯、软软的毛毯、还有滴答走动的小钟。这些平平常常的物件，原来都悄悄藏着家人的爱。

她忽然很想唱歌。于是轻轻哼了起来——哼歌像一朵云朵做的被子，把小宝宝软软地裹住，哼歌里还藏着摇篮曲的节拍，一下，又一下。

小宝宝，你听见了吗？那阵温柔的哼歌声，是妈妈在替月亮妈妈和你说悄悄话呢。叮铃铃，连风铃也踮起脚，来给这首歌伴唱。

月亮妈妈忽然明白了：原来「我是谁」并不重要，重要的是，有这么多声音和颜色，都在陪着你慢慢长大。

早安，小宝宝，今天的世界亮晶晶的。等你准备好了，外面的风会很软，月光会很暖，我们都在轻轻地，等你。

✨ 故事小语
爱是有声音也有颜色的，每一份温柔都在悄悄告诉你：你被好好地爱着，慢慢长大就好。

The Little Alarm Clock's Lullaby (Prenatal)

On the windowsill sat a tiny alarm clock named Ding-dang, who loved nothing more than singing to the baby still asleep in Mama's tummy. Tonight he had a wonderful secret to share.

He whispered a soft tune, hush, hush, and listened. From the next room came Daddy's boom-boom snore, low and safe like distant thunder. Ding-dong, Ding-dang tapped gently, keeping the beat.

Grandma's hands were knitting by the lamp, click-clack, click-clack, weaving warm little socks. The way home was short, but it was full of sounds that meant 'you are loved.'

Ding-dang leaned close to the round little tummy and said in his tiny ticking voice: little one, can you hear it? That boom-boom, boom-boom — that is Mama's heartbeat, the very first song written just for you.

He discovered something wonderful: every family sound was a lullaby. The hum of the light, the creak of the floor, even the quiet — all of it was the world saying welcome.

So he sang his goodnight the only way he knew: ding-dong, time to sleep. And the baby, still small and unseen, listened and grew safe and sound in the warm, singing dark.

✨ 故事小语
Every little sound of a family is a kind of lullaby — love is all around you, even before you are born.
