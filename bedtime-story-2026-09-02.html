小鸟啾啾的轻轻远行（胎教期）

一天早晨，小鸟啾啾站在屋檐上，忽然想去看一看风会带它去哪里。它把翅膀展开，跟着一缕软软的风，出发了。小宝宝，风来了，你感觉到了吗？它正轻轻摇着妈妈窗台上的风铃，叮叮当，叮叮当。

风带着啾啾飞过金色的田野。麦浪哗啦哗啦地起伏，像一片会唱歌的海洋。啾啾落在最高的麦穗上，麦穗轻轻弯下腰，像在向它问好。「你好呀，小客人，」麦穗说，「你要去哪里呀？」「我也不知道，」啾啾说，「我就想看看柔软的风景。」

风又带着啾啾飞过一条小溪。溪水汩汩地流着，把阳光揉碎成千万片亮晶晶的小镜子。啾啾低头一看，水里有自己的倒影，圆圆的、小小的，随着水波一荡一荡。「原来我在水里是这个样子呀。」啾啾笑了，声音落在水面上，荡开一圈圈小涟漪。

傍晚的时候，风累了，把啾啾轻轻放在一座小山顶上。山下的房子亮起一盏盏暖黄的灯，像撒了一把会发光的种子。啾啾望着那些灯，忽然想家了，想屋檐下的那个小窝，想窗台上那个看它出发的小宝宝。

「风呀风，我们回去吧，」啾啾说，「外面的风景再好看，我也想把它们讲给家里的小宝宝听。」风点点头，呼地一下，又把啾啾托起来，往家的方向飘。月光洒在它们身上，圆圆的，软软的，像一盏小夜灯，安安静静地陪着它们回家。

回到屋檐下的时候，月亮已经挂在天上了。啾啾钻进暖和的窝里，把今天看见的金色田野、闪亮小溪和小山上的灯光，一样一样收进心里。「小宝宝，」它轻轻说，「等你准备好了，我带你去看看那片金色的田野。现在，你先在妈妈怀里，安心长大就好。」

小宝宝，你听见了吗？世界在窗外轻轻地等着你。等你准备好了，风会来接你，月光会照着你，我们会一直陪着你，就像月亮陪着啾啾回家那样。晚安，小宝宝。

✨ 故事小语
世界很大很美，但无论走多远，心里都装着家，装着要等的你。

Dodo the Cloud and the Four Seasons (Prenatal)

Dodo the little cloud spirit lived in the cloud cotton field, light as a feather and soft as a hug. "Float, float, home at last," Dodo would say, drifting from cloud to cloud. But today Dodo thought, "Let me float down and take care of the seasons."

Spring came first. A soft drizzle went plip-plop, plip-plop on the leaves. "The little seeds are thirsty," said Dodo, and it stretched itself thin and wide, catching the rain like a gentle umbrella, letting it fall in tiny, careful drops. The seeds drank and drank, and sighed a happy little sigh.

Summer came next, hot and bright. "Oh dear," said Dodo, "the flowers are getting sleepy in the sun." So it floated over them like a soft white hat, and the flowers nodded, cool and happy, while a little bee went tick-tack against the petals, thanking Dodo with a song.

Then came autumn, and the leaves turned red and gold. "They are getting tired," said Dodo. "Let me carry them home." One by one, it floated the falling leaves to a cozy spot under the big tree, where they could sleep all winter, snug as bugs in a blanket.

Winter arrived, white and quiet. Dodo gathered the coldest clouds and fluffed them into the softest snow, then wrapped the little tree roots in a warm white blanket. "Sleep warm," whispered Dodo. "Spring will come back."

When Dodo floated home that night, a ring of warm light like open arms held it steady, as if in a soft little nest. "Float, float, home at last," Dodo smiled. "And every season is a different kind of hug."

Little one, while you grow safe and sound in Mommy's tummy, the seasons are all getting ready for you: the spring rain, the summer sun, the autumn leaves, and the winter snow. And every one of them will hold you gently, the way everyone who loves you will. Just grow safe and sound.

✨ 故事小语
Every season holds its own gentle warmth, just like every hug from the people who love you.
