小闹钟叮当的午夜乐队（胎教期）

窗台上的小闹钟叮当，最爱在深夜办一场小小的音乐会。它轻轻晃着身体：叮咚，该睡啦。

淅淅沥沥，窗外下起了细细的小雨，像有人在轻轻敲着小鼓。叮当竖起耳朵，觉得这雨声真好听。

一只小猫跳上窗台，咕咕地打着呼噜，像一把小小的暖壶。叮当和它一唱一和，雨声、呼噜声，正好凑成一支午夜乐队。

扑棱扑棱，窗外的树叶也被吵醒了，抖落几颗亮晶晶的雨珠。叮当悄悄把闹钟的指针拨慢一点点，好让这场音乐会多响一会儿。

小宝宝，你听到了吗？这些轻轻的声音，都是夜里的小精灵在给你唱摇篮曲呢。

后来雨停了，月亮探出头，把银色的光铺在叮当身上。它打了个小小的哈欠：晚安，小宝宝，月亮陪着你。

✨ 故事小语
夜晚不是黑黑的，它藏着许多轻轻的声音陪着你；安心睡吧，世界很温柔。

The Seed Who Counted the Stars (Prenatal)

Deep in Mama's garden, a tiny seed named Sprout waited under the warm soil. She was patient, and she had a happy little secret.

Above her, the earth turned slowly — ding-dong went a faraway bell, and fizz went the little bubbles in the dew. The whole round world was spinning, carrying her like a cradle.

Sprout listened to the Sahara's starry sky through the dark: lub-dub, lub-dub, that was Mama's heartbeat, steady as a drum. 'Just a little longer,' Sprout whispered to the baby above.

She dreamed she grew into a small tree whose leaves were made of the coral reef's colors, and whose shade was the Grand Canyon's quiet book. The baby in the tummy would one day read it, page by page.

When morning came, a single drop of rain tapped her shell: tick, tick. Sprout smiled. She was exactly where she needed to be.

Little one, grow slow and safe. The stars are counting you, and the garden is waiting — good night, tiny traveler, good night.

✨ 故事小语
Good things grow slowly and gently — just like you, little one, taking your time in a world full of wonder.
