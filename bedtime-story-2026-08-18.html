风铃的夏天（胎教期）

屋檐下挂着一串风铃，细细的银管，一碰就叮叮当当响。这个夏天，风铃可忙了，它想把每一阵路过的风，都编成一支歌。

早晨的风来啦，轻轻柔柔的，风铃叮——地应了一声，像刚睡醒的问候。中午的风热乎乎的，风铃叮叮咚咚，像在说：慢慢走，别着急。

傍晚的风最温柔，带着晚饭的香味，从窗户缝里钻进来。风铃响得轻轻的，生怕吵醒了正在妈妈肚子里打盹的小宝宝。

一天，风铃问风：你整天东奔西跑，累不累呀？风笑着回答：不累，因为我要把你的歌，送到很远很远的地方去。

小宝宝，你感觉到了吗？风从窗户缝里溜进来，带着风铃的叮当声，像妈妈的手，一下一下，轻轻地拍着你。

夜深了，风停了。风铃安安静静地挂在屋檐下，可它心里还藏着白天的歌。它想，等小宝宝出生了，一定要把这支歌，唱给他听。

宝宝呀，这个夏天有风，有风铃，有叮叮当当的歌。而你，就是这些歌里，最被期待的那一个。

✨ 故事小语
风会来，风也会停，可歌会一直留在心里。你听见的每一声叮当，都是这个夏天在轻轻跟你说话。

The Wind Chime's Summer (Prenatal)

Under the eaves hangs a wind chime, with thin silver pipes that ring at the slightest touch. This summer, the chime is busy — it wants to turn every passing wind into a song.

The morning wind comes, soft and gentle. The chime answers — ding — like a sleepy greeting. The noon wind blows warm, and the chime rings ding-ding-dong, as if saying: go slowly, no hurry.

The evening wind is tenderest, carrying the smell of dinner through the window crack. The chime rings very softly, careful not to wake the little one dozing in mama's belly.

One day the chime asks the wind: you rush about all day — are you not tired? The wind laughs: no, because I carry your song to far, far places.

Little one, can you feel it? The wind slips through the window, carrying the chime's ding-ding, like a mother's hand, patting you, soft and steady.

Night falls, and the wind stops. The chime hangs quiet under the eaves, but it still holds the day's songs in its heart. When you are born, it will sing them for you.

Little one, this summer has wind, and chimes, and ding-ding songs. And of all those songs, you are the one most hoped for.

✨ 故事小语
The wind comes and goes, but the song stays in your heart. Every ding you hear is summer speaking to you softly.
