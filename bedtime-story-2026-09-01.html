小海螺的软软上午（胎教期）

清晨的阳光像蜂蜜一样流进海草森林，把每一片海草都涂得亮晶晶的。小海螺小旋旋从壳里探出两个小小的触角，软软地伸了个懒腰。小宝宝，你也会伸懒腰吗？像小海螺一样，把手臂伸得长长的，舒舒服服的。

小旋旋想用身体好好感受这个上午。它把软软的小肚子贴在海床上，咦，沙子是凉凉的、滑滑的，还带着一点点痒。「原来沙子在给肚子挠痒痒呀！」小旋旋忍不住笑出声，咕噜咕噜滚了一圈，又咕噜咕噜滚回来。

它又伸出小触角，轻轻碰了碰身边的海草。海草是滑溜溜的，像妈妈的裙摆一样轻轻晃。「叮叮当，叮叮当。」海草互相碰着，发出细细的声音。小宝宝，你听见了吗？那是海草在唱歌。

小旋旋走呀走，遇见一只趴在珊瑚上的小虾米。小虾米挥着长须说：「你来啦！快看，太阳把这块珊瑚照得暖乎乎的，你也来坐一坐呀。」小旋旋把身体贴在珊瑚上，真的，热热的、暖暖的，像贴着一个小太阳。

「原来暖乎乎是这样的感觉呀。」小旋旋满足地说。它又用小触角点了点自己的壳，壳是硬硬的、圆圆的，像一个小小的家。「有壳真好啊，走到哪里，家就到哪里。」

午后的阳光慢慢斜了，小旋旋回到自己的壳里，把小触角收起来，只留下一个小小的圆孔。它舒舒服服地打了个哈欠，用软软的声音说：「今天的世界，是凉凉的、滑滑的、暖暖的。」

小宝宝，等你准备好了，外面的世界也有许多软软的东西等着你：柔软的草地、温暖的阳光、还有轻轻抱着你的风。它们都亮晶晶的，像今天上午的海草森林一样。现在，安心在妈妈怀里长大就好，世界会一直等你。

✨ 故事小语
用身体慢慢感受世界的软与暖，就是幸福最朴素的样子。

🔬 🔬科学故事（胎教期）：萤火虫的小灯笼

夏天的晚上，河边草丛里亮起一盏一盏小小的灯，忽明忽暗，像星星落到了地上。小萤火虫阿灯提着它自己的小灯笼，在草叶间轻轻地飞。小宝宝，你也看见那些小小的光点了吗？那是萤火虫在跟你打招呼呢。

阿灯飞累了，落在一片狗尾巴草上，低头看着自己的肚子。那团小光正一闪一闪地亮着，像一颗有呼吸的小星星。「为什么我的肚子会发光呢？」阿灯好奇地想，「别的虫子的肚子都不亮呀。」

它问路过的蟋蟀。蟋蟀摇摇头：「我是用翅膀唱歌的，你听，蛐蛐蛐蛐。发光的事，我不懂。」阿灯又问水里的青蛙。青蛙呱呱笑着：「我是用声音找朋友的，呱呱呱。发光的事，你该去问老月亮。」

老月亮挂在河面上，听见阿灯的问题，笑呵呵地说：「小萤火虫，你的肚子里住着一间小小的灯房，叫发光器。灯房里有荧光素，还有荧光素酶。你轻轻一吸气，空气里的氧气就跑进来，它们一见面，就开心地发出光来。」

「原来是这样呀，」阿灯把尾巴翘起来，又使劲吸了一口气，「呼——」它的肚子更亮了，像一个圆圆的、温柔的小灯笼。「可是，」阿灯又问，「为什么我们要发光呢？」

老月亮说：「光是你们的悄悄话呀。你一亮，远处的萤火虫朋友就看见了，知道你在哪里，知道你想找它玩。你们用光打招呼、找朋友，就像蟋蟀用歌声、青蛙用呱呱一样。」阿灯听了，心里暖暖的，它把光调得又软又亮，一闪，一闪，像在说：嗨，我在这里。

小宝宝，等你准备好了，夏天的晚上你也会看见萤火虫的小灯笼。它们的光柔柔的、暖暖的，却几乎不发热，人们叫它「冷光」——那是萤火虫跟同伴打招呼、找朋友的悄悄话。现在，安心在妈妈怀里长大，等夏天来了，我带你看萤火虫去。

✨ 故事小语
萤火虫的肚子会发光，是因为它的腹部藏着发光器，里面有荧光素和荧光素酶，遇到空气里的氧气就会发出柔和的光；这种光几乎不发热，人们叫它「冷光」，是萤火虫跟同伴打招呼、找朋友用的。

Little Chirp's Bag of Sounds (Prenatal)

Drip went the rain on a shiny green leaf. Pitter-patter went the tiny feet of a cat. Chirp, chirp, chirp went little Chirp the bird, watching from the eaves. Swoosh went the wind, and a little white feather floated down, right onto Chirp's wing.

"Oh!" said Chirp. "What a lovely morning! I will fill my little bag with sounds. One for the rain. One for the wind. One for the cat's pitter-patter feet. Then I will sing them all to the little baby growing in Mommy's tummy."

So Chirp flew low, and the rain went drip, drip, drip on the leaf, and into the bag it went — plip! Then the wind went swoosh through the willow tree, and into the bag it went — whoosh! Then a big bee went buzzzzz, and into the bag it went — buzz!

"More, more!" chirped Chirp, flapping its wings. It flew over the garden, where the fountain went plip-plop, plip-plop. It flew over the path, where soft paws went pad, pad, pad. Every little sound went into the bag, until the bag was round and full and singing.

When evening came, Chirp perched on the windowsill and opened the bag just a tiny bit. Out came the drip, the swoosh, the buzz, the plip-plop, and all together they made the softest lullaby. "Hush," sang Chirp, "little one, can you hear? The whole world is humming you a good night."

Then Chirp wrapped itself in a cloud-soft little blanket on the warm sill and closed its eyes. "Tomorrow I will find new sounds," it said, "and I will keep them all for you."

Little one, when you are ready, the world will play you the same music: the drip of rain, the buzz of bees, the pad of little feet, and the warm hum of everyone who loves you. Sleep safe and sound. The song is already waiting for you.

✨ 故事小语
Even the softest sounds are little gifts of love when we listen together.

🔬 🔬 Science Story (Prenatal): Little Lantern's Light

On a summer night, tiny lights blinked over the river, soft and golden. One of them was Little Lantern, a small firefly, and its light went blink, blink, blink as it flew. Little one, can you see the tiny lights in the dark? They are fireflies saying hello.

Little Lantern landed on a blade of grass and looked down at its own belly. The little light was blinking there, like a star with a heartbeat. "Why does my tummy glow?" it wondered. "Nobody else in the grass glows."

It asked the cricket. The cricket shook its head: "I sing with my wings — chirp, chirp. I know nothing about glowing." It asked the frog. The frog laughed: "I call my friends with my voice — ribbit, ribbit. For glowing, ask the old moon."

The old moon floated on the river and heard Little Lantern's question. It smiled: "Little firefly, inside your belly is a tiny lamp room. It holds a little powder called luciferin, and a helper called luciferase. When you take a breath, oxygen from the air runs in, and when they meet, they burst into soft light."

"So that is it," said Little Lantern, and it took a deep breath. Whoosh! Its belly glowed rounder and brighter, like a little golden lantern. "But why do we glow?" it asked again.

"The light is your quiet hello," said the old moon. "When you blink, faraway firefly friends see you. They know where you are, and that you want to play. You say hello with light, just as the cricket says hello with song and the frog with ribbit, ribbit." Little Lantern's heart felt warm. It made its light soft and bright — blink, blink — like saying: hi, I am here.

Little one, when you are ready, you will see fireflies on summer nights. Their light is soft and warm, but it barely makes heat at all — people call it "cold light." It is a firefly's way of saying hello and finding friends. Now, grow safe and sound in Mommy's tummy, and when summer comes, we will watch the fireflies together.

✨ 故事小语
A firefly's belly glows because of a light organ inside it — a chemical called luciferin meets oxygen with the help of an enzyme, and makes a soft, cool light that fireflies use to say hello and find friends.
