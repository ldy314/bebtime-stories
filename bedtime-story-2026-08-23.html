影子的朋友（胎教期）

傍晚，太阳斜斜地照进屋子，墙上出现了一个安静的小影子。它跟着光，慢慢地移动，像一条温柔的小河，汩汩地流。

小影子不吵，也不闹。白天，它躲在脚下；傍晚，它爬上墙，变成一只小黑猫，变成一座小山，变成一条会游的小鱼。风从窗缝钻进来，呼——呼——地逗它。

它陪着窗边的绿植，陪着桌上的水杯，陪着妈妈一起叠衣服。妈妈的手一抬，小影子也抬起手；妈妈低下头，小影子也低下头。衣服叠好了，发出沙沙的轻响。

天一点一点暗下来。小影子的颜色慢慢变淡，最后，它轻轻地说：我回家啦，明天傍晚，我还会来陪你。

小宝宝，你感觉到了吗？星星们手拉手，围成一个小小的摇篮，轻轻摇着你，像小影子一样，安静地守着你。

夜里，小影子不见了。可它其实哪儿也没去，它就藏在光的后面，等第二天太阳出来，再轻轻巧巧地爬回墙上。

宝宝呀，这世上有一种陪伴，是安安静静的。它不说话，却一直都在——就像影子，就像家里每一个等你出生的人。

✨ 故事小语
有些陪伴是安静的，不说话，却一直都在。就像影子，就像正在等你出生的家人。

The Shadow Friend (Prenatal)

In the evening, the sun slants into the room, and a quiet little shadow appears on the wall. It moves with the light, slowly, like a gentle river, rippling soft.

The little shadow makes no sound and never fusses. In the daytime it hides under your feet; at evening it climbs the wall and becomes a little black cat, a small hill, a fish that swims. The wind slips through the window crack — whoosh — teasing it.

It keeps the potted plant company, and the cup on the table, and mama as she folds clothes. When mama raises a hand, the shadow raises a hand; when mama lowers her head, the shadow lowers its head too. The clothes settle with a soft rustle.

The sky darkens bit by bit. The little shadow fades, and at last says softly: I am going home now. Tomorrow evening, I will come to keep you company again.

Little one, can you feel it? The stars join hands into a tiny cradle and rock you gently, like the little shadow, keeping watch over you in silence.

At night the little shadow is gone. But it has not gone far — it hides behind the light, and when the sun returns, it slips quietly back onto the wall.

Little one, there is a kind of company that is quiet. It says nothing, yet is always there — like a shadow, like everyone at home waiting for you to be born.

✨ 故事小语
Some company is quiet; it says nothing, yet is always there. Like a shadow, like the family waiting for you to be born.
